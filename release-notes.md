# MusicViz v2.2.1

**Release date:** 2026-09-27

The first release as MusicViz: a music-first YouTube Music client for Android TV, forked from
[Flow](https://github.com/A-EDev/Flow) by A-EDev.

## New features

- Music is the home of the app: your YouTube Music home with mood chips and every shelf, in
  YouTube Music's order, loaded all the way to the end
- Quick picks, Long listens, Trending songs and Covers and remixes shelves now appear (they
  were dropped before)
- Sign in with your phone to see your own YouTube Music home; signed out you get the regular
  YouTube Music home
- Search always opens on Music, with Videos as the second mode for live sets and concerts
- Background playback is on by default

## Changes

- The video Home and Subscriptions tabs are removed; the rail is Music, Search, Library and
  Settings
- New app id `nl.neerdael.musicviz`: MusicViz installs as its own app next to Flow
- About credits Flow as the project this app is built on
